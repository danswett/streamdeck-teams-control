Moves the deck between the three bundled profiles as a meeting goes: Teams Meeting when you join, PowerPoint Live (Attendee) or (Presenter) while a deck is up, back to Teams Meeting when it ends, and back to your own profile when you leave. Needs the bundled profiles, which Stream Deck offers to install only when following first needs them. An unconfirmed change pauses following across restarts instead of repeatedly asking to install. Turn this off and on to retry. Shared setting — every key in this plugin uses it.
