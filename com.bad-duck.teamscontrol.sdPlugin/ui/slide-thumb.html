Off by default. This is one of the few parts of the plugin that reads what is on a slide rather than which buttons exist. With it on, the slide is copied from the Teams window to your Stream Deck several times a second while you are presenting.

Everything stays on this computer. Nothing is uploaded, sent anywhere, saved to disk, or written to any log — the picture goes to the deck on your desk and is replaced by the next one. Turning this off drops it immediately. Leave it off if you present material that should not appear on an external display.

On a Mac, turning this on asks macOS to allow Stream Deck under Screen Recording, which is how the picture is taken off the Teams window. It is asked for only here, separately from Teams control, and needs macOS 14 or later.

Lets the plugin press Teams' meeting controls for you. Turning it on asks macOS to allow Stream Deck under Accessibility. Every Teams key needs it. Shared setting: every key in this plugin uses it. Slide thumbnails need Screen Recording as well, asked for separately by the slide dials.
