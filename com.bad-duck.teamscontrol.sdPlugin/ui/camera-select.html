Video preferences apply when this button selects a camera, including one already selected. Leave unchanged preserves Teams' current setting. These are global Teams settings, not device defaults; changes made manually or with other buttons are not automatically overridden. Soft focus changes only the toggle, not its strength. Mirroring opens the Video effects and settings pane locally.

Listed by Windows, including virtual cameras. No meeting is needed to configure this button. Switching requires a Teams meeting and the device to be available in Teams. If you reconnect a webcam to a different USB port, refresh and select it again. No other webcam is used as a fallback.
