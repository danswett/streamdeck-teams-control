Off by default, because this is one of the few parts of the plugin that reads what is on a slide rather than which buttons exist. With it off the key shows a slide icon and the count, which is all it has ever shown.

Everything stays on this computer. The picture is copied from the Teams window straight to your Stream Deck. Nothing is uploaded, sent anywhere, written to disk, or recorded in any log. Leave this off if you present material that should not be shown on an external display.

The picture is taken when you change slide, so a build or a pen stroke mid-slide will not appear until you move on. The touch-strip thumbnail on a Stream Deck + XL follows those as they happen.
