Press opens, hold confirms

Teams confirms this itself, so the key follows suit: a press opens its "Stop presenting?" dialog, and keeping the key held answers it. Let go early and the dialog stays up to be answered on screen. Stopping ends the presentation for everyone in the meeting.

Moves the deck between the three bundled profiles as a meeting goes: Teams Meeting when you join, PowerPoint Live (Attendee) or (Presenter) while a deck is up, back to Teams Meeting when it ends, and back to your own profile when you leave. Stream Deck offers to install the bundled profiles only when following first needs them. An unconfirmed change pauses following across restarts instead of repeatedly asking to install. Turn this off and on to retry. Shared setting — every key in this plugin uses it.
