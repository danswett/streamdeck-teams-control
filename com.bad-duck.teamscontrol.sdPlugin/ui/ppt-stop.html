Press opens, hold confirms

Teams confirms this itself, so the key follows suit: a press opens its "Stop presenting?" dialog, and keeping the key held answers it. Let go early and the dialog stays up to be answered on screen. Stopping ends the presentation for everyone in the meeting.

Moves the deck between the three bundled profiles as a meeting goes: Teams Meeting when you join, PowerPoint Live (Attendee) or (Presenter) while a deck is up, back to Teams Meeting when it ends, and back to your own profile when you leave. 15-key Stream Decks. Shared setting — every PowerPoint Live key uses it.
